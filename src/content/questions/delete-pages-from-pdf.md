## Mac (Preview)

1. **View > Thumbnails**.
2. Select the page(s) to remove (Cmd+click for several).
3. Press **Delete**.
4. **File > Save** (or Export as PDF for a new file).

## iPhone and iPad

In **Files**, open the PDF, show thumbnails, long-press a page > **Delete**.

## Windows (free)

**Print to PDF, keeping only the pages you want:**
1. Open the PDF in Chrome or Edge > Ctrl+P.
2. Destination: **Save as PDF**.
3. Pages > Custom: list the pages to **keep**, e.g. `1-4, 6-10` removes page 5.

Or use an online "delete PDF pages" tool, where you click the pages to remove.

## Adobe Acrobat Pro

Tools > **Organize Pages** > select > trash icon > Save.
