## Two different goals

1. **Convert each page into an image.** Most common, e.g. to post a page on social media or insert it into a slide.
2. **Extract the images inside the PDF.** Get the original photos out at their embedded resolution.

## Mac (Preview)

1. Open the PDF in Preview and select the page in the sidebar.
2. **File > Export…**
3. Format: **JPEG** or **PNG**. Set resolution (e.g. 150 or 300 pixels/inch).
4. Save. Repeat for other pages.

## Windows

- **Screenshot:** zoom the page to fill the screen, press **Win+Shift+S**, and select the area. Quick for one page.
- **Online converter:** upload the PDF and download a ZIP of all pages as JPGs.
- **Adobe Acrobat (paid):** File > Export To > Image > JPEG.

## iPhone and Android

- Take a **screenshot** of the page in your PDF viewer.
- Or use an online PDF-to-JPG tool in the browser.

## Extracting embedded images

- **Online "extract images from PDF" tools** pull out originals.
- **Adobe Acrobat Pro:** Export PDF > Image > *Export all images*.
- **Command line (free):** `pdfimages -all input.pdf prefix` from the Poppler utilities.
