## Step 1: Is the form fillable?

Open the PDF and click where the answer goes. If a blinking cursor or highlighted box appears, it's **fillable**. Many viewers also highlight the fields or show a banner saying "This document contains interactive form fields".

## Filling a fillable form

- **Chrome/Edge:** open the PDF, click fields, type, then click the **download/save** icon.
- **Mac Preview:** click fields and type; File > Save.
- **Adobe Acrobat Reader:** click and type; File > Save.
- **iPhone:** open in **Files** or **Mail** and tap the fields.
- **Android:** open with **Adobe Acrobat Reader** or **Google Drive's PDF viewer**, which supports forms.

## Filling a non-fillable (flat) form

- **Adobe Acrobat Reader:** **Fill & Sign** > click anywhere to add text, checkmarks, dates.
- **Mac Preview:** **Markup** > **T** (Text) and drag the text box into place.
- **Edge:** **Add text**.
- **iPhone Files:** Markup > **+** > **Text**.

## "Please wait…" or a blank form

It's an **XFA form**. Browsers, Preview and phone apps can't display it. Download the file and open it in **Adobe Acrobat Reader** on Windows or Mac. See [PDF forms explained](/learn/pdf-forms/).

## Before you submit

- Reopen the saved file to confirm your answers are there.
- [Sign](/questions/sign-a-pdf/) if required.
- Consider **flattening** (Print > Save as PDF) so answers display the same everywhere.
