## Mac (Preview)

1. Open the PDF in **Preview**.
2. Click the **Markup** (pen) icon > **Sign** icon > **Create Signature**.
3. Sign on the **trackpad**, hold a signature on paper up to the **camera**, or use your iPhone.
4. Click the signature to place it. Drag to position and resize.
5. **File > Save.**

## Windows (Adobe Acrobat Reader, free)

1. Open the PDF in **Acrobat Reader**.
2. Choose **Fill & Sign** > **Sign yourself** > **Add signature**.
3. **Type**, **Draw** or **Image** (upload a photo of your signature).
4. Click to place it, then save.

## Windows (Microsoft Edge)

Open the PDF in Edge > **Draw** > sign with a mouse, pen or touchscreen > **Save**. Handy but less neat than Acrobat's signature tool.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open the PDF in **Files** (or from Mail).
2. Tap the **Markup** (pen) icon > **+** > **Add Signature**.
3. Sign with your finger, tap **Done**, then position it.

## Android

Use the free **Adobe Acrobat Reader** app: **Fill & Sign** > signature icon > create and place.

## Need signatures from others?

E-signature services (DocuSign, Adobe Acrobat Sign, Dropbox Sign and others) send the document, collect signatures in order, and produce an audit trail. Learn more: [Electronic vs digital signatures](/learn/electronic-vs-digital-signatures/).
