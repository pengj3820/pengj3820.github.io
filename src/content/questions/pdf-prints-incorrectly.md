## Quick fixes by symptom

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Content is cut off at the edges | Choose **Fit** / **Shrink oversized pages**; check the paper is A4 vs Letter |
| Prints too small | Choose **Actual size** (100%) instead of Fit |
| Prints sideways or wrong orientation | Tick **Auto-rotate and center**; [rotate pages](/questions/rotate-pdf-pages/) |
| Blank pages or missing images | Update your viewer; try Acrobat's **Print as image** |
| Garbled text or wrong fonts | Fonts not embedded. Try another viewer or Print as image |
| Very slow printing | Huge images. [Compress the PDF](/questions/how-to-compress-a-pdf/) first |
| Print button greyed out | Printing restricted by the author |
| Form entries don't print | Flatten the form or use Acrobat Reader; check *Document and markups* is selected |

## Adobe Acrobat Reader tips

- **Page Sizing & Handling:** Fit / Actual size / Shrink oversized pages / Multiple (several pages per sheet) / Booklet.
- **Comments & Forms:** choose *Document and Markups* to include annotations and filled fields.
- **Advanced > Print as image:** fixes most garbled-output issues, at the cost of speed.

## A4 vs Letter

Europe and most of the world use **A4** (210 × 297 mm); the US and Canada use **Letter** (8.5 × 11 in). Printing one on the other clips edges or leaves margins. **Fit** or **Shrink oversized pages** handles this.
