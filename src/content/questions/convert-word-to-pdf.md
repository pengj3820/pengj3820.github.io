## Microsoft Word (Windows)

1. **File > Save As**, then choose a location.
2. Set **Save as type** to **PDF (*.pdf)**.
3. Optional: **Options…** to choose page range, create bookmarks from headings, or make it PDF/A.
4. Choose **Standard** (printing) or **Minimum size** (online), then **Save**.

## Microsoft Word (Mac)

**File > Save As** > File Format: **PDF** > choose *Best for electronic distribution and accessibility* (keeps links and tags) > **Export**.

## Google Docs

**File > Download > PDF Document (.pdf)**.

## Pages (Mac/iPhone)

**File > Export To > PDF**.

## On a phone

- **Word app:** tap **…** > **Export** or **Share** > **PDF**.
- **iPhone, any app:** Share > **Print** > pinch outward on the preview > Share > Save to Files.
- **Android, any app:** Share/Print > choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.

## Tips for a perfect PDF

- Use **Save As/Export**, not "Print to PDF", to keep links, bookmarks and accessibility tags.
- Use **headings** (Heading 1, 2) in Word to get automatic PDF bookmarks.
- Embed fonts if you used unusual fonts. See [PDF fonts look wrong](/questions/pdf-fonts-look-wrong/).
- Large images make large PDFs. Compress pictures in Word first (Picture Format > Compress Pictures).
