## Method 1: Print to PDF (any computer, free)

1. Open the PDF in **Chrome** or **Edge** (drag it into a browser window).
2. Press **Ctrl+P** (Windows) or **Cmd+P** (Mac).
3. Destination: **Save as PDF** (Chrome) or **Microsoft Print to PDF** (Edge).
4. Pages: choose **Custom** and enter e.g. `1-3` or `2, 5, 8-10`.
5. Save. Repeat for each part.

## Method 2: Mac Preview

1. Open the PDF > **View > Thumbnails**.
2. Select the pages you want (Cmd+click).
3. **Drag them to the desktop.** Preview creates a new PDF with just those pages.

## Method 3: iPhone and iPad

1. Open the PDF in **Files** and show thumbnails (swipe from the left edge or tap the sidebar icon).
2. Long-press a page. You can delete or rotate pages, then save a copy.
3. Or use **Print**, select a page range, pinch outward, then Share > Save to Files.

## Method 4: Split into many files

- **Online splitters:** split by range, every N pages, or file size.
- **PDFsam Basic** (free, offline): *Split* module > "Split after every N pages" or "Split by size".
