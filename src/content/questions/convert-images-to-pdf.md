## Windows 10 and 11 (built-in)

1. Select the images in File Explorer (Ctrl+click for several).
2. Right-click > **Print** (in Windows 11: *Show more options* > Print).
3. Printer: **Microsoft Print to PDF**. Choose a layout (Full page photo) and untick *Fit picture to frame* to avoid cropping.
4. Click **Print** and choose where to save.

## Mac (Preview)

1. Select the images in Finder > right-click > **Open With > Preview**.
2. Drag thumbnails in the sidebar to reorder.
3. **Edit > Select All**, then **File > Print** > **PDF** (bottom-left) > **Save as PDF**.

Faster: select images in Finder > right-click > **Quick Actions > Create PDF**.

## iPhone and iPad

1. In **Photos**, select the images > **Share** > **Print**.
2. **Pinch outward** on the preview to open it as a PDF.
3. Tap **Share** > **Save to Files**.

Or in the **Files** app: select images > **…** (More) > **Create PDF**.

## Android

1. Open the image in **Google Photos** or your gallery.
2. **Share/⋮ > Print** > choose **Save as PDF** as the printer.
3. Tap the PDF download button.

For multiple pages, use Google Drive's scan feature or an online JPG-to-PDF tool.

## Online (any device)

Free JPG-to-PDF converters let you drag and drop multiple images, reorder them, pick page size and margins, and download a single PDF.

**Photographing paper documents?** A scanner app gives much better results than plain photos. See [Scan a document to PDF with your phone](/questions/scan-document-to-pdf-with-phone/).
