## Mac (Preview)

1. Open the PDF and show thumbnails: **View > Thumbnails**.
2. Select the page(s).
3. Press **Cmd+R** (right) or **Cmd+L** (left).
4. **File > Save**. The rotation is now permanent.

## Windows (Microsoft Edge)

1. Open the PDF in **Edge**.
2. Click the **Rotate** button in the toolbar. It rotates all pages.
3. Click **Save**.

For single pages on Windows, use an online rotate tool or PDFsam Basic.

## iPhone and iPad (Files app)

Open the PDF, show thumbnails, long-press a page > **Rotate Left/Right**. Changes are saved automatically.

## Adobe Acrobat

- **Reader (free):** View > Rotate View affects only your screen.
- **Acrobat Pro:** Tools > Organize Pages > rotate > Save.

## Prevent sideways scans

Most phone scanner apps auto-rotate. Check the preview before saving. See [Scan a document with your phone](/questions/scan-document-to-pdf-with-phone/).
