## Method 1: Microsoft Word (best quality)

Works in Word 2013 and later on Windows, and recent versions on Mac.

1. Open Word, then **File > Open**, and choose the PDF.
2. Click **OK** when Word says it will convert the PDF.
3. Review the result and save as **.docx**.

✅ Keeps most formatting · ✅ Runs locally (private) · ✅ OCRs scans

## Method 2: Google Docs (free, any device)

1. Upload the PDF to **Google Drive**.
2. Right-click it > **Open with > Google Docs**.
3. Choose **File > Download > Microsoft Word (.docx)**.

✅ Free · ✅ OCR included · ❌ Loses most layout, images and tables

## Method 3: Online converters

Adobe, Smallpdf, iLovePDF and others offer free PDF-to-Word conversion, often with better layout retention than Google Docs. Free tiers may limit the number of conversions per day. Avoid uploading confidential documents.

## Method 4: LibreOffice (free, offline)

LibreOffice opens PDFs in **Draw** by default, which is good for small edits, but it doesn't produce a flowing Word document. For true conversion, Word or an online tool is better.

## Tips for better results

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Scanned PDF comes out as images | Make sure OCR runs (Word/Google Docs), or [OCR the PDF first](/questions/make-scanned-pdf-searchable/) |
| Tables break apart | Convert to **Excel** instead, or use an online PDF-to-Excel tool |
| Fonts change | Install the missing fonts, or accept substitutes |
| You only need a few paragraphs | Just [copy the text](/questions/cant-copy-text-from-pdf/) |
