## Mac (Preview, built-in)

1. Open the first PDF in **Preview**.
2. **View > Thumbnails** to show the sidebar.
3. Drag other PDFs from Finder **onto the thumbnail sidebar**, where you want them to go.
4. Reorder pages by dragging thumbnails.
5. **File > Export as PDF** to save the combined file.

Faster: select PDFs in Finder > right-click > **Quick Actions > Create PDF**.

## iPhone and iPad (Files app)

1. Open **Files** and go to your PDFs.
2. Tap **…** > **Select** and choose the files, in order.
3. Tap **…** (More) > **Create PDF**.

## Windows

Windows has no built-in merge tool. Free options:

- **Online merge tools:** drag in your files, reorder, then download.
- **PDFsam Basic** (free, open source, offline): *Merge* module > add files > Run.
- **Adobe Acrobat** (paid): Tools > Combine Files.

## Android

Use an online merge tool in Chrome, or a PDF app from the Play Store with a merge feature.

## Tips

- **Name files 01, 02, 03…** so they sort correctly.
- If files are different page sizes (A4 and Letter), that's fine. Each page keeps its size.
- **Password-protected PDFs** must be unlocked before merging.
