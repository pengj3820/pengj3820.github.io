## Common attachment limits

| Service | Limit per email |
|---|---|
| Gmail | 25 MB |
| Outlook.com / Hotmail | 20 MB (larger files via OneDrive link) |
| Yahoo Mail | 25 MB |
| iCloud Mail | 20 MB (up to 5 GB with Mail Drop) |
| Corporate Exchange servers | Often 10 MB |

Remember: encoding adds ~33%, so aim for files **well below** the limit.

## Way 1: Compress it

Most oversized PDFs are scans or contain photos and shrink dramatically. Follow [How to compress a PDF](/questions/how-to-compress-a-pdf/) with the medium setting.

## Way 2: Send a link instead

- **Gmail:** click the Google Drive icon when composing.
- **Outlook:** attach from OneDrive, or Outlook offers to upload big files automatically.
- **Apple Mail:** Mail Drop kicks in automatically for large attachments.
- Or upload anywhere (Dropbox, WeTransfer) and paste the link.

Check the sharing setting is "Anyone with the link" if the recipient isn't in your organisation.

## Way 3: Split it

Use a [PDF splitter](/questions/split-pdf/) to send "Part 1 of 3", "Part 2 of 3"… Useful when the recipient's portal accepts only attachments.

## Way 4: Zip it (small help)

ZIP compression helps little on PDFs, because they are already compressed. Expect only 0–10% savings. Only worth it to bundle many files together.
