## iPhone and iPad (built-in)

**Files app:**
1. Open **Files**, go to a folder, tap **…** (top-right) > **Scan Documents**.
2. Hold the phone above the page. It captures automatically when it detects the edges.
3. Scan more pages, then tap **Save**. You get a multi-page PDF.

**Notes app:** create a note > tap the camera icon > **Scan Documents**. Share > Save to Files to export the PDF.

## Android (Google Drive)

1. Open **Google Drive** and tap **+** (or the camera icon) > **Scan**.
2. Photograph the page. Drive crops and enhances it.
3. Tap **+** to add pages, then **Save** as PDF.

## Other free scanner apps

- **Adobe Scan:** automatic OCR, so the result is searchable.
- **Microsoft Lens:** scan to PDF, Word or PowerPoint.

## Tips for a perfect scan

- **Lighting:** bright, even light and no shadow from your phone or hand.
- **Contrast:** place white paper on a dark surface so edges are detected.
- **Hold flat and parallel** to the page.
- **Choose the filter:** *Black & White* or *Greyscale* for text (smaller, sharper), *Color* for photos.
- **Check page order and rotation** before saving.
